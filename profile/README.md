# Amulet Laboratories

A small software studio based in Eureka, California. Run by [Andrew Passanisi](https://andrewpassanisi.com).

Two kinds of work I offer:

- **Build** — websites and custom software for small businesses (hotels, coffee shops, therapy practices, dispensaries, and other local operators). Fixed quote, real code, you keep everything.
- **Audit** — accessibility, architecture, and design-system audits for product teams that already have a Vue 3 codebase. Fixed-fee, written reports committed to your repo, walkthrough call to close. Monthly advisory retainer for ongoing input.

→ **[amuletlabs.org](https://amuletlabs.org)** — studio site, services, demos, blog, audit offerings

## What's public here

- **[Rig](https://github.com/Amulet-Laboratories/Rig)** — Headless, accessible Vue 3 component library. 149 components across 12 packages, completely unstyled, styled via `data-rig-*` attributes. Used as the foundation for every site we ship.

The rest of the studio (the marketing site, the personal site, in-progress products, internal tooling) is in private repos.

## How we work

- **Fixed quotes, not hourly.** You see the price before any work starts. If scope changes, we re-quote — in writing, before a keyboard is touched.
- **No platform lock-in.** Your code lives in your GitHub. Your domain is registered to you. Your site is deployed on your account, paid by you, owned by you. You can leave whenever you want, with everything.
- **WCAG 2.2 AA by default.** Not a feature of the proposal. The proposal.
- **One person from start to finish.** The person who quotes you is the person who builds it. No handoffs, no junior team, no agency layer.

The longer version of any of this is on [the studio blog](https://amuletlabs.org/blog).
